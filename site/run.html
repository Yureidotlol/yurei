This page deploys Yurei and plays one full story with real transactions: an honest answer that a seal acts on, an executor that lies and is caught in a few rounds, and a second executor that answers the reopened question. Every step links to the explorer.

01 · Your wallet

Your wallet only funds the run and receives everything back. It signs two transfers; it is never asked to approve a contract.

Demo parameters
02 · Role wallets

The protocol needs separate parties: a requester, two executors and a watcher. This page creates four throwaway wallets for them in your browser. Their keys stay on this device. Download the backup before funding, so nothing is lost if you close the tab.

RoleAddressETHUSDG
03 · Fund and run
These contracts are a research prototype and have not been audited. The amounts are kept tiny on purpose. Yurei is open, so anyone may answer or challenge: if a stranger catches the planted lie before our watcher does, they keep the liar's 1 USDG bond. That is the protocol working.
04 · Finish

Return everything to your wallet. The seal's unspent USDG can be reclaimed once the seal expires; it always goes to your wallet, and anyone may trigger it.

Every transaction on this page is real and public. Contract sources: github.com/Yureidotlol/yurei
